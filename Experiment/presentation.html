PRESENTATION RUNBOOK

My reasoning. My device.

A live feasibility demonstration of the HE-Tech proposal: a private argumentation sandbox with learner-reviewed interpretation and independent formal checks.

Prepare on each device

  1. Use Device setup to consent to the download and install all assets.
  2. Run the full benchmark, including Gemma. Record the device label and charging/battery condition. Collect at least three cold-worker and three warm-worker runs under comparable conditions; cold workers keep installed assets. Export the recent runs, including failures and blocked tests. Warm Gemma in the reasoning workspace while reading the scenario if desired.
  3. Install to the home screen. Enable cache-only mode. On mobile, turn on airplane mode, close and reopen the installed app.
  4. Use a fresh attempt. Keep a saved JSON backup. Allow several minutes for first-use GPU compilation.

A cached screen is not evidence of offline inference: submit and verify a new thought after disconnecting.

Five-minute reasoning demonstration

  1. Control: open Preferences, choose guidance and reduce visual detail. Explain that the optional timer pauses during processing and never locks the learner out.
  2. Review: submit “If the asked guard is a liar, their direct answer about this door matches its safety.” Check Gemma’s interpretation before confirming. The false reasoning should receive a counterexample.
  3. Refine: submit “If I ask whether the other guard would say this door is safe, the answer is opposite to the safety of this door, whichever guard I ask.” Confirm only if the interpretation matches.
  4. Separate knowledge and action: show that the scenario remains unfinished. Knowing which door is safe does not automatically supply the learner’s choice rule.
  5. Choose: submit “For that question, I go through the other door if and only if the answer is yes.” Review both answer branches and confirm.
  6. Inspect: open Inspect logic & code, then the graph view and both engine sources. Show the closed kernel theorem, original player quotation and goal check.
  7. Reflect: open the debrief: exploration, the direct route, transferable concepts. Write a reflection and export it.

If interpretation is inaccurate, demonstrate correction rather than confirming it. Translation fidelity is itself an evaluated part of the prototype.

Show transfer and authoring

Choose among three murder investigations of increasing complexity. Explain that a suspicion score is not a conviction. Import a bundled THY example and inspect its propositions. Download and edit the small custom THY template to author a new case.

Browser investigation proofs are conditional on their finite rule representation. The problem-only HOL theory is shown as reference; browser verification does not execute arbitrary imported Isabelle code.

Engineering tests establish feasibility on the tested device. They do not establish educational effectiveness, accessibility for every learner, or performance on untested phones.

Procedural university challenge: The Evidence Review

Open Choose scenario, then The Evidence Review. Choose Researcher skills, Campus exhibition, Stretch challenge and seed manchester-2026 for a reproducible demonstration. Review two records, explain a warrant and disposition for each, identify the limits, and consider the separate counterfactual. Guidance stays adjustable.

For paired transfer practice, keep the seed and difficulty and change the subject or context. Research misconduct review is an optional setting containing allegations and professional scrutiny; neutral settings remain available. A justified referral is distinct from establishing misconduct. Generated THY contains the problem and rules without a worked solution.

Feature coverage v6 checks all 84 subject/setting/difficulty combinations, sampled proofs, procedural UI, and four real Gemma inputs when model checks are selected. The generator remains outside the 18 fixed player-journey scripts. Use the current exported report for this device; sampled success does not validate every possible learner explanation.

Guided onboarding and a three-stage investigation

From the start screen or scenario library, choose the guided archive tutorial. Move through the brief, use an editable example if helpful, review its interpretation and establish the two connections. The debrief separates explanation, reflection and transfer into short optional pages.

For an evidence-allocation demonstration, choose The Evidence Review, Three stages, Researcher skills, Campus exhibition and a saved seed. Establish the current stage's bounded report, then choose which check to fund. An independent comparison costs two of the three credits; an explanation audit costs one. The next brief is prepared locally while reading. A result may challenge an expectation, and preserving uncertainty is valid when the evidence remains incomplete.

Use Hide scenario information to concentrate on the thinking workspace. Between stages, add a short reflection or skip it; at the end, inspect the complete stage history. Export a reasoning record to preserve the choices and reflections. The feature benchmark covers tutorial interpretation, stage boundaries, deterministic paths and portable restore. These implementation checks do not assess learning gains.
